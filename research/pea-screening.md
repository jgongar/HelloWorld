# PEA screening: leads where consensus fair value is well above price

**As of:** 18 October 2026. **Universe:** listed companies headquartered in the EU or EEA (PEA-eligible), all sizes, excluding banks, insurers, REITs and pre-revenue biotech. **Style:** quality growth, tilted towards medium-sized niche players, supply-chain bottlenecks, chemicals and "alternative" themes.

> **Read this first.** This is a list of leads to research further. It is not investment advice and not a valuation. It was built only from free sources reached through web search, because this environment blocks direct access to financial data sites (Yahoo Finance, MarketScreener/Zonebourse, Boursorama, Euronext, etc.). Every price and target below carries its date. Several are from June–August 2026, and Q3 results season (late Oct–Nov) will move many of them. **Check the live price and consensus before acting on any line.**

---

## 1. How the screen works

**Thresholds (as agreed):** market cap ≥ €50m, ≥ €100k traded per day, ≥ 2 analysts, plus a minimum upside to the average analyst target that depends on size:

| Size bucket | Market cap | Minimum upside to consensus |
|---|---|---|
| S | < €1bn | ≥ 35% |
| M | €1–5bn | ≥ 25% |
| L | > €5bn | ≥ 15% |

**Signals checked for each company:**
- Upside to the average (or median) analyst target.
- **Consensus trend:** which way targets and estimates have moved over the last 1–3 months.
  - ↑ = raised or upgraded
  - → = stable or mixed
  - ↓ = cut
- Growth outlook.
- Balance sheet.
- Market position and moat.
- Exposure to a structural theme.

**Tiers:**
- **A:** meets the threshold, consensus rising, and the quality-growth case is intact. Best starting point.
- **B:** meets the threshold, consensus stable or mixed, and the thesis is intact.
- **C:** meets the threshold, but consensus is falling, or the company is cyclical, value-type, high-risk or a special situation. The price may have fallen faster than the targets ("falling knife" risk).

**Lead types** (tags used in the tables):
- **Revisions↑:** analysts are moving up.
- **Supply-chain:** a niche bottleneck supplier to a high-demand market (AI chips, defence, GLP-1 drugs, radiopharma, grid, rail, offshore wind).
- **Derated quality:** a high-quality business sold off on a narrative, often the 2026 fear that AI will disrupt software.
- **Cheap multiples:** low valuation multiples.
- **Hidden assets:** net cash, a holding discount, or a sum-of-the-parts gap.
- **Niche leader:** global or European #1 in a narrow market.

**Coverage:** about 200 companies were checked one by one, and about 140 were rejected (see §6). The full working log is in [`pea-screening-raw-log.md`](pea-screening-raw-log.md).

## 2. Market context that shaped the results

- **The AI hardware supply chain has already re-rated.** Technoprobe is up ~170% YTD, LU-VE has rallied on a hyperscaler contract, and AT&S is near highs. Most data-centre cooling and power names (LU-VE, Carel, Mersen, Nexans, NKT, Cenergy) now trade within 10–22% of their targets, so they fail the threshold.
  - [UBS SMID list via Investing.com](https://fr.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/les-derniers-ajouts-dubs-a-sa-liste-paneuropean-small--midcap-3638484)
  - [LU-VE / TP ICAP](https://www.teleborsa.it/News/2026/10/12/lu-ve-tp-icap-midcap-alza-target-price-backlog-rafforza-ulteriormente-la-visibilita-sul-2027-41.html)
- **The February 2026 "AI eats software" sell-off.** It created the clearest gaps between price and target in quality software and data names: Scout24, Nemetschek, Atoss, Lectra, Equasens and Ipsos.
  - [Euronews](https://euronews.com/business/2026/02/14/software-sector-selloff-which-european-companies-are-hit)
  - [Morningstar](https://global.morningstar.com/en-gb/markets/after-selloff-where-next-european-software-stocks)
- **Defence slumped in 2026 after the 2024–25 run.** Upside has reopened in second-line names such as Renk, Invisio and Steyr, while targets are still being cut.
  - [Admiral Markets on Rheinmetall](https://admiralmarkets.com/education/articles/shares/rheinmetall-stock-2026-slump)
- **Chemicals:** Q2-26 brought the first year-on-year price increase in three years, but the 2026 Iran conflict pushed up costs. Commodity chemicals (Kemira, Lanxess, Wacker) still fail; specialty niches with their own drivers (Alzchem, AMG, Nabaltec) pass.
  - [Barclays via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/barclays-warms-to-eu-downstream-and-defensive-chemicals-upgrades-2-stocks-4854349)
- **Valuation backdrop:** French small caps traded around 10× 2027 earnings versus about 15× for the MSCI France index in May 2026, per Bloomberg as cited by Investing.com.
  - [Investing.com](https://fr.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/ces-6-small-caps-francaises-de-qualite-pourraient-senvoler-jusqua-94-3433508)
- **A structural caveat:** sell-side targets are optimistic on average, and most stocks show some upside to consensus at any time. That is why the thresholds are high and why the *direction* of revisions matters as much as the gap.

## 4. Lead cards

Each card covers: **what the company does**, **why it is a lead**, **what to watch**, and **sources**.

### 4.1 AI / semiconductor and electronics supply chain

**1. Jenoptik (DE, Tier A)**
- **What it does:** Photonics group. Makes optical modules for chip equipment (a key supplier into the lithography chain), metrology, and smart-mobility and defence optics.
- **Why a lead:** The clearest positive-revision story in the screen. Warburg raised its target from €32 to €46, DZ Bank from €46 to €48, and Deutsche Bank to €52 Buy in October. The share fell to €37.6 on 15 Oct, about 11% below the 6-Oct level, so the gap widened while targets rose. AI and chip demand is cited as the driver of the 2026 outlook.
- **Watch:** Exposure to the chip-equipment cycle; Q3 results on 10 Nov.
- **Sources:** [DB €52](https://goldesel.de/aktien/news/jenoptik-aktie-deutsche-bank-hebt-kursziel-auf-52-euro-und-bestatigt-buy) · [consensus](https://www.boerse-express.com/boerse/aktie/jenoptik-ag-na-on+tts-149064561) · [DZ €48](https://goldesel.de/aktien/news/jenoptik-aktie-profitiert-dz-bank-erhoht-kursziel-auf-48-euro-ki-und-chip-nachfrage-starkt-die-2026-aussicht)

**2. Technoprobe (IT, Tier A, large)**
- **What it does:** Italian world-class maker of probe cards used to test chips on the wafer, including HBM memory and AI accelerators.
- **Why a lead:** Even after +170% YTD and entry into the FTSE MIB, targets keep rising: Goldman Sachs went from €40 to €44, Equita to €44 (8-Oct, assuming significant capacity growth), Deutsche Bank €45. Equita models €2.4bn sales and €1.3bn EBITDA by 2028.
- **Watch:** Very strong momentum, so a cyclical drawdown would be violent. Large shareholders have placed stock at a discount before.
- **Sources:** [Equita 8-Oct](https://www.borsaitaliana.it/borsa/notizie/teleborsa/finanza/technoprobe-equita-alza-target-price-e-prevede-aumento-della-capacita-significativo-95_2026-10-08_TLB.html?lang=it) · [Investing consensus](https://www.investing.com/equities/technoprobe-consensus-estimates)

**14. SUSS MicroTec (DE, Tier B)**
- **What it does:** Lithography, wafer-bonding and coating tools. Hybrid/temporary bonding is a direct play on HBM and chiplet advanced packaging.
- **Why a lead:** About 35% upside to a €105 consensus (UBS €105.5, Jefferies €109, Berenberg €125) at roughly 14× forward earnings. Deutsche Bank upgraded to Buy in August.
- **Watch:** Q2 orders were pulled forward, so Warburg expects a softer second half. EPS revisions have been negative since mid-July, and DZ Bank rates it Sell.
- **Sources:** [finanzen.net / UBS](https://www.finanzen.net/analyse/suss_microtec_se_ex_s_ss_microtec_buy-ubs_ag_1098118) · [Investing consensus](https://www.investing.com/equities/suess-microtec-n-consensus-estimates)

**15. AT&S (AT, Tier B)**
- **What it does:** High-end PCBs and ABF IC substrates for AI processors; plants in Austria, China and Malaysia.
- **Why a lead:** Median target €215.5 (16-Oct) against about €152, so +42%. Oddo upgraded it to Outperform at €220 on 4-Sep. The 2026/27 guide is still +30–35% revenue growth at a 25–29% EBITDA margin.
- **Watch:** The guidance appears trimmed from +45–55%, free cash flow is clearly negative (heavy capex), and coverage is small with one Sell.
- **Sources:** [Börse Express consensus](https://www.boerse-express.com/analyse/at-s-aktie-kaufen-oder-verkaufen) · [Oddo upgrade](https://goldesel.de/aktien/news/ats-aktie-oddo-bhf-stuft-auf-outperform-hoch-und-hebt-kursziel-auf-220-euro)

**16. Kontron (AT, Tier B)**
- **What it does:** Embedded and IoT computing, train-to-ground and defence communications, industrial software.
- **Why a lead:** 13–19 analysts, almost all Buy, average target €28–31 against about €20.6, so +37–49%. Cheap for a business with defence and rail exposure.
- **Watch:** The price date is not confirmed. The group is acquisitive, and the quality of its cash flow should be checked.
- **Sources:** [aktien.guide](https://aktien.guide/kursziel/Kontron-AG-AT0000A0E9W5)

**17. VusionGroup (FR, Tier B)**
- **What it does:** Electronic shelf labels and in-store IoT/data platform (the Walmart rollout).
- **Why a lead:** Down about 38% over 12 months; median target €180–213 against €129.6 (Aug).
- **Watch:** Aggregator coverage is thin (2 recorded views) and the figures conflict. Concentration on one large customer.
- **Sources:** [Idéal Investisseur](https://www.ideal-investisseur.fr/avis-analystes-consensus/vusiongroup/FR0010282822.html)

**18. Nabaltec (DE, Tier B, small)**
- **What it does:** Mineral-based flame retardants (aluminium hydroxide) for cables, including data-centre cabling; boehmite for EV battery separator coatings; technical ceramics.
- **Why a lead:** +44% to a €16 target; order backlog +36%, with demand from data centres cited.
- **Watch:** EBIT margin reset to 5–7%; small and thinly covered.
- **Sources:** [Goldesel](https://goldesel.de/aktien/news/nabaltec-aktie-analyst-bestatigt-kaufempfehlung-und-kursziel-von-16-euro) · [aktien.guide](https://aktien.guide/kursziel/Nabaltec-DE000A0KPPR7)

## 7. Method, limitations and next steps

**How the list was built:**
1. Searched broker top-pick lists and screening articles by country and theme (UBS, Kepler, Jefferies, Intesa, French, German and Nordic press).
2. Built a list of about 200 PEA-eligible candidates biased to niche quality growth and to your themes (AI/semiconductor, grid and data-centre, defence, GLP-1, radiopharma, critical minerals, rail, offshore wind, specialty chemicals, plus derated quality).
3. Checked each candidate for price, consensus target, number of analysts and recent target changes.
4. Applied the thresholds, tiered the survivors and wrote the cards.

**Limitations to keep in mind:**
- **Not a full mechanical crawl.** Direct access to data sites is blocked in this environment, so names were found through search rather than by scanning every listed company. Under-followed micro-caps are certainly missed.

  - Copenhagen: ALK-Abelló
  - Oslo: Cadeler, Medistim
  - Vienna: DO & CO, Palfinger, AT&S
